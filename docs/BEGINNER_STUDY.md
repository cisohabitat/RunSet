# First-time-player study (manual verification)

Status: paused by the user; not run. Automated lesson completion is not a substitute for testing with
people who have never played tile-rummy. No participant outcomes are claimed.

Recruit five first-time players across desktop, iPhone, and iPad. Use participant
IDs rather than names. Ask each to think aloud, without explaining the rules or
pointing at controls. Record assistance separately from successful completion.

Tasks:

1. Explain the objective after viewing Home.
2. Find Learn to play and complete the basics lesson.
3. Explain what makes a group, a run, and a 30-point opening.
4. Try an incorrect selection and recover using the feedback.
5. Finish the joker and rearranging exercises, explaining what changed.
6. Start the real Easy game; complete an opening play or correctly draw if no
   opening is available. Use Hint and explain why its proposed move is legal.
7. Leave and continue the game, export a backup, then find the delete action and
   cancel it. Ask what “Saved” and “Ready offline” mean.

Target: at least four of five complete their first regular turn without verbal
coaching. Record time, mistaken actions, control-discovery problems, readability,
and any point where assistance was needed. Revisit the worst recurring problem
before adding features.
