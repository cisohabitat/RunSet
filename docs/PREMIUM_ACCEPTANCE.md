## Physical Apple devices

- Small iPhone Safari: portrait and landscape, browser bars shown/hidden, safe
  areas, native View selector, scrolling populated melds, rack and primary action.
- iPad Safari landscape, portrait and Split View: long runs, 50-tile table,
  selection, touch scrolling, optional larger tiles and comfortable reading.
- Full screen/Table only: rack restoration after leaving view, new turn and reload;
  Escape with an attached keyboard; Settings and native confirmation focus.
- VoiceOver and enlarged text: tile colour/number/shape, selected state, latest-play
  mark, invalid group/run explanation, destination and Check move names.
- Offline first visit: Chinese, rules, all lessons, exercises and a numbered
  puzzle after Ready offline; service-worker update with a saved timed game.
- Audio on real hardware: first tap after lock/unlock, silent-mode behavior,
  headphones, interrupted playback, hidden tab, source/language changes and mute
  versus deliberately requested narration. Confirm Stop and text always suffice.

## Older Chinese-speaking beginners

Use the five-participant protocol in [BEGINNER_STUDY.md](BEGINNER_STUDY.md),
without supplying hints verbally. Ask participants to explain groups, runs,
opening 30, joker replacement/reuse and safe Reset in their own words. Include
finding Quick exercises, using a clue, rejecting an unfinished joker turn,
replaying one puzzle number and explaining that records stay on this device.

Ask native Mandarin listeners whether number sequences, 同数组, 顺子, 万能牌,
开局 and 摸牌 are clear and correctly pronounced. Compare the recorded guide and
the device voice at a comfortable volume. Record warmth, cheerfulness, pace and
comprehension separately; +3 Hz pitch and −5% rate alone do not establish these.
Retain exact caption/audio filenames with any correction request.

Acceptance target: at least four of five beginners finish their first real turn
without verbal coaching; explain any assisted completion separately. Track
mis-taps, unread text, lost destinations, uncertainty about saved progress and
voice fatigue. Fix recurring problems, then retest them on the same device class.
